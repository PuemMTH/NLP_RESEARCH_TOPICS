  • Why it works: Connects your NECTEC OCR work to her Public Health IE focus. It solves the "Thai-specific" and "Medical-domain" challenges.

Topic 2: Spatiotemporal Disease Tracking via Multimodal Social Sensing

  • The Idea: Combine text-based surveillance (her core work) with visual data (e.g., images from social media or news) to improve the granularity of disease outbreak detection.
  • Why it works: Leverages your CV skills (Classification/Detection) to enhance her surveillance frameworks.

Topic 3: Efficient Multilingual Medical ASR/OCR for Cross-Border Health

  • The Idea: Building upon her Myanmar/Thai work, develop a unified, lightweight pipeline for cross-border medical document digitizing.
  • Why it works: Uses your engineering skills (Go/Docker/FastAPI) to make her research operational and accessible.
