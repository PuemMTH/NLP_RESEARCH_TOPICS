## Addendum: Thai Medical OCR + Post-correction

- Open question: Should post-correction operate at character level or token level for Thai medical terminology with mixed Thai-English text?
- Research direction: Build a "medical term preservation" metric alongside CER/WER to prevent clinically harmful corrections.
- Research direction: Evaluate compact VLM OCR against classic OCR + language model correction under the same latency and privacy constraints.
