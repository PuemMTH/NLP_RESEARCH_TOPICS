## Research Trend Outlook (2026-2028) (2026-04-05)

### Direction likely to accelerate
- Safety-and-verification layers for language-conditioned agents (robotics and structured extraction pipelines).
- Data-efficient adaptation (LoRA/partial tuning/distillation) for domain and embodiment shift.
- Multilingual deployment tracks (Thai-English code-switch, Thai-Myanmar low-resource transfer).

### Direction likely to slow down
- Pure demo-driven end-to-end claims without recovery/safety analysis.
- OCR-only pipelines for complex medical layouts without region/layout understanding.
- Accuracy-only reporting that ignores field-level reliability and operational risk.

### High-probability contribution patterns
- Hybrid systems: foundation model + constraints/ontology/rules.
- Evaluation-focused work: robustness, safety violations, recovery time, and cost-performance trade-offs.
- Domain transfer: applying surveillance/event-extraction knowledge to modern low-resource health-information tasks.

### Practical thesis implication
- Best risk-adjusted line: choose one deployment domain, then contribute one cross-cutting method:
	safety constraint, multilingual grounding, or efficient adaptation.
